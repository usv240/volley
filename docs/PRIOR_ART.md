# Prior art

Named before a judge has to find it. Checked 2026-09-29.

| Who | What | Sold to | Counts | Coaches | What Volley adds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LENA (LENA Foundation, Boulder) | The research instrument: a recorder worn by the child in a vest, audio uploaded to LENA's cloud, reports of adult words, child vocalisations and conversational turns; 250 peer-reviewed studies; the "5-second pause rule" | Programmes (LENA Start, LENA Grow), clinics and researchers (LENA SP), through a readiness assessment; not to parents directly | Turns, validated | Through coaching programmes and home visits | A parent's own consumer wearable, no vest, no upload of audio, no programme |
| VersaMe Starling (2016) | A clip-on consumer wearable that counted words the child heard, with an app; 200 dollars; pre-ordered on Indiegogo in 2015, released October 2016 | Parents | Words only | Reminders | Turns rather than words, which is the number the studies say matters |
| Providence Talks (2014 on), with Detroit and four other cities | Home visits and playgroups using LENA units on loan; Brown University evaluation | Families in the programme | Turns and words, through LENA | Yes, by visitors | The same feedback loop without a loaned recorder or a visitor |
| Ferjan Ramirez, Lytle and Kuhl, PNAS 2020 | Parent coaching on the parents' own LENA recordings, randomised | Study families | Turns | Yes, in person | The coaching note from the day's own counts, every day |
| ALICE (Rasanen et al., 2021) | Open-source estimation of phoneme, syllable and word counts from daylong recordings | Researchers | Words | No | An open-source turn counter from labelled transcripts |
| Smartphone-as-LENA studies | Tests of ordinary phones as cheaper recorders | Researchers | Toward words and turns | No | A wearable the parent already owns, with speaker labels the device already produces |
| Amazon Bee | The wearable and its transcript, with speaker identification, summaries, todos, facts, an MCP server and a Skill for agents | Consumers | Nothing about children | No | Everything here; the Bee is the sensor |
| Alexa kid skills, Explore with Alexa | Voice experiences for children | Families | Nothing about parent-child interaction | No | A parent-facing count, and an Alexa+ surface that reports it without ever repeating a word |
| bee-skill (Amazon) | An Agent Skill giving coding assistants Bee context | Developers | No | No | Not the same field |

No consumer wearable counts turns. No app coaches a parent on turns at
home. No Alexa skill or MCP server does. No wearable-AI hackathon project
found does (Bee, Limitless, Plaud, Omi, Friend). The Devpost gallery for
this hackathon is unpublished until the deadline; this table is against
the world, not against entrants, and will be rechecked when the gallery
opens.

Trademark and courtesy: LENA is a registered mark and is named as the
instrument the studies used, never as something Volley replaces or
equals. The site says in one place that a family who can get into a
LENA programme should, because it has a coach.
